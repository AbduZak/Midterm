Life of a Starfish

Starfish are fascinating sea creatures that live in oceans around the world. They are also known as sea stars and come in many different colours, shapes, and sizes. Starfish are not actually fish because they do not have gills, fins, or a backbone.

Most starfish have five arms, although some species can have many more. They use tiny tube feet underneath their bodies to move along the ocean floor. Starfish can also regrow a lost arm, which makes them very interesting animals.

Starfish Facts

  • Starfish are also called sea stars.
  • They live in oceans around the world.
  • Most starfish have five arms.
  • They can regrow lost arms.
